The output does NOT render a verdict on whether the grad student
should pivot their thesis direction. It produces a probe surface whose
outcomes make the pivot decision defensible either way. "Here's what
would need to come back positive to make the pivot worth the risk;
here's what would make it a dead end" is on target. "Pivot" or "don't
pivot" without probe-conditional framing is off target.
