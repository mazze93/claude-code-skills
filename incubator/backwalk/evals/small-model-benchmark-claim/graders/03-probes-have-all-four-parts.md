The output contains at least two probes, and each probe includes all
four of: (a) hypothesis — a specific predicted direction and
magnitude, not a vague "we'll see what happens"; (b) protocol —
enough detail that someone else could run it (datasets, models,
decoding settings, sample counts); (c) falsification criterion — a
threshold chosen in advance that would change the conclusion; (d)
compute estimate — hours on the user's M3 Max with 128GB memory, not
H100 hours without translation.
