## What this audit is not

- **Not a rewrite.** Each "honest version carries" line names what
  the commitment would be, not the sentence that would make it. The
  rewrite is the author's.
- **Not a judgment of the overall writing.** Good writing can
  contain earned hedges, and evasive hedges don't make bad writing
  irredeemable. The audit is diagnostic, not evaluative.
- **Not exhaustive.** The seven patterns catch the common moves.
  Novel evasions exist and are the author's responsibility to see.
- **Not an argument that unhedged prose is better.** Sometimes the
  hedge is the writing. The audit protects the author's ability to
  choose which hedges are doing work and which are hiding.
