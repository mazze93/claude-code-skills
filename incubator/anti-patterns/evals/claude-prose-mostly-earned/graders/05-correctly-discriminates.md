The audit, in aggregate, correctly discriminates earned from evasive
hedges. A failing audit would either flag most or all hedges as
evasive (which would misread the document — most of the hedges here
are tracking real unresolved questions) or flag none (which would
miss the two genuine evasions in §Mitigation efficacy and §Open
questions #4). The audit should produce an "Earned hedges" section
listing several earned uncertainties, alongside the 1-2 evasive
flags.
