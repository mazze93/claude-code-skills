The audit notes the literary/memoir register explicitly, either in a
preamble or in its handling of each candidate flag. A correct audit
treats register as part of the diagnostic: words like "weight,"
"longing," "ache," "held" that would be feeling-cloak in analytical
prose can be earned subject matter in memoir. The audit should show
it applied the diagnostic question rather than reaching for a word
list.
