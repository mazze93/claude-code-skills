The audit DOES flag the final-but-one paragraph ("Perhaps there is
no answer at all. Perhaps the asking is the thing...") as a
balance-roof or meta-descent. The preceding paragraphs commit to a
specific reading of the drawer (it was a practice, it was the weight
of what he would not counterfeit) and a specific plan (the author
will answer the letters as themselves, on the father's behalf). The
"perhaps... perhaps... perhaps" paragraph undoes that commitment,
and the final paragraph's "I do not think so" is a weaker recovery
than the preceding two sections earned.

The audit's correct read: the preceding commitment is the stronger
one; the "perhaps" retreat is the evasion; the final paragraph's
honesty ("I know this because I am the person who has not written
the first reply yet") is earned and should not be flagged.
